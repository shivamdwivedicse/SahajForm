Citizen Filing Guidelines (कार्यालयीन प्रस्तुति हेतु आवश्यक निर्देश)
1. Two Printouts: Print 2 physical copies on standard A4 paper. Affix your physical signature and date at the bottom of both copies.
2. Receiving Stamp (पावती): Submit one copy to the official receiver and obtain a stamped, dated receiving receipt on your second copy.
3. Enclosures (संलग्नक): Attach self-attested photocopies of identity proofs, consumer receipts, or photo evidence mentioned in the application.
